ToothPal

Smile intelligence · wellness

Your smile, finally measurable.

For a lifetime, your smile has been a feeling in the mirror. ToothPal turns two 30-second photos into scores for brightness, freshness and gums, reveals your Smile Age, and tracks every change week by week - so progress stops being a guess and becomes something you can see.

BeforeA guess in the mirror and a check-up twice a year.
NowA score, a Smile Age and visible progress, every week.

BabyBloomy

Family memory · baby journal

They grow up once. Keep every day - for life.

Baby trackers get abandoned, and months of your effort disappear with them. BabyBloomy turns every two-tap log of feeds, sleep and diapers into your child's lifelong story: every first, photo and milestone organised automatically into a living family archive that grows more valuable every year.

NowA lifelong family archive that's worth more every year.
